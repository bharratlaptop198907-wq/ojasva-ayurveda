Scientific Testing & Phytochemical Research

Every OJASVA formulation undergoes a rigorous 5-step quality verification system to guarantee chemical purity, potency, and safety.

Our 5-Step Quality Verification Protocol

Merging traditional Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia standards with modern analytical testing.

1. DNA Barcoding of Botanical Raw Materials

We authenticate the exact plant species prior to processing, eliminating adulterants or species confusion.

2. Heavy Metal ICP-MS Testing

Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry testing ensures zero contamination from lead, mercury, cadmium, or arsenic.

3. Bio-Active HPLC Standardization

High-Performance Liquid Chromatography verifies exact concentrations of active ingredients (e.g., Withanolides in Ashwagandha).

4. Pesticide & Pathogen Screen

Comprehensive screening guarantees 100% freedom from agricultural pesticides, mycotoxins, micro-organisms, and synthetic additives.

5. Bio-Availability & Clinical Trials

Formulations undergo double-blind observational safety and efficacy clinical studies across partner Ayurvedic institutes.
