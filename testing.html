What We Test For in Every Batch

No batch leaves our facility without passing all 4 independent safety benchmarks.

Heavy Metals Screen

ICP-MS Tested

We test to parts-per-billion (ppb) limits for Lead, Mercury, Arsenic, and Cadmium to strictly satisfy WHO & AYUSH safety guidelines.

  • ✓ Lead: < 2.0 ppm
  • ✓ Mercury: < 1.0 ppm
  • ✓ Arsenic: < 3.0 ppm

Microbial Contamination

USP <61> / <62>

Screens for harmful micro-organisms, yeast, mold, E. coli, and Salmonella to guarantee complete purity and long shelf-life safety.

  • ✓ Total Aerobic Count: < 10,000 cfu/g
  • ✓ E. coli & Salmonella: Absent

Pesticides & Residues

GC-MS/MS Screen

Analysis across 200+ common agricultural pesticides, fungicides, and herbicides ensuring 100% clean herbal sourcing.

  • ✓ Multi-Residue Screen: Non-Detectable
  • ✓ Glyphosate: Non-Detectable

Bio-Active Potency

HPLC Assay

Standardized High-Performance Liquid Chromatography ensures that active compounds meet guaranteed percentage thresholds.

  • ✓ Withanolides (Ashwagandha): > 5.0%
  • ✓ Curcuminoids (Turmeric): > 95.0%
